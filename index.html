    FAQ

    Questions people ask us

    How much will it cost?

    It depends on what you need. Send us the details and we'll reply with a clear quote, free of charge.

    Yes. Explain your idea in your own words, in Sinhala or English, and we'll turn it into a plan.

    How long does a project take?

    A simple website can take days. Systems and POS take longer. We give you a timeline with the quote.

    Can you help with my university project?

    Yes. We build and guide student projects, and we explain every part so you can present it with confidence.

    Do you help after the project is finished?

    Yes. If something breaks or you want a change, message us and we'll sort it out.
